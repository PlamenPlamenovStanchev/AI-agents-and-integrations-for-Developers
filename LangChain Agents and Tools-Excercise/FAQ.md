# Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept the payment methods displayed at checkout. Payment must be completed before an order is processed or shipped.

## 2. When will my order be shipped?

Orders are normally processed within the timeframe shown on our website. Shipping times may vary based on product availability, destination, carrier service, and seasonal demand.

## 3. Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

Please contact us as soon as possible. We will try to accommodate changes or cancellations, but we cannot guarantee them once an order has entered processing or has shipped.

## 4. How can I track my order?

When tracking is available, we will send a shipping confirmation with tracking information after your order has been dispatched.

## 5. What is your return policy?

Eligible items may be returned within the return period stated on our website, provided they are unused, in their original condition, and include original packaging where applicable.

## 6. When will I receive my refund?

After we receive and inspect an approved return, refunds are issued to the original payment method. Your bank or payment provider may require additional time to post the refund.

## 7. What should I do if my item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Contact our customer support team promptly with your order number and clear photos of the item and packaging. We will review the issue and provide the appropriate resolution.

## 8. Are product prices and availability guaranteed?

Prices and availability may change without notice. We make reasonable efforts to keep product information accurate, but we reserve the right to correct errors and cancel affected orders when necessary.

## 9. How do you protect my personal information?

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information. We collect and use data in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

## 10. How can I contact customer support?

Use the contact details or support form listed on our website. Please include your order number, if applicable, so we can assist you more efficiently.
